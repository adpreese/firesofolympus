Congratulations Blacksmiths\! You have been summoned by Zeus to prove your skill and worth. You will be sent on 3 Quests to find the best among you. Each Quest, Blacksmiths craft a weapon from their chosen Ingredients and then face two monsters, to Challenge the *Strength* and *Sharpness* of their blades.

## Game Structure

To start each Quest, the Strength Challenge and Sharpness Challenge decks are each shuffled and the top cards are revealed face up. Then Ingredients are drawn from the top of the Ingredients deck and placed in the middle of the table face up.

## Crafting Your Weapon

Blacksmiths take actions to select Ingredients, Sharpen their weapon, or Pass. Actions are taken in a snake draft order(Blacksmiths Andrea, Bob, Carlos, Dhivya would act in the following order: Andrea, Bob, Carlos, Dhivya, Dhivya, Carlos, Bob, Andrea, etc). The order for the first Quest is decided by rolling 2d6, with highest going first. If there are ties, those Blacksmiths re-roll. For the second and third Quests, order is decided by the number of Successes from the previous round with the Blacksmith with the fewest Successes going first. Players with equal numbers of Successes roll 2d6 to determine order with highest going first. Once a Blacksmith has zero or fewer Action Points, they may not take any more actions.

Actions are to select an Ingredient, Sharpen your weapon, or Pass. Starting Action Points for each Quest are as follows:

| Quest 1 | 3 Ingredients drawn per Blacksmith | 3 Action Points |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Quest 2 | 4 Ingredients drawn per Blacksmith | 4 Action Points |
| Quest 3 | 4 Ingredients drawn per Blacksmith | 5 Action Points |

Action: Sharpening your weapon may be done on any turn and costs one Action Point. You may sharpen up to three times and it has the following payoffs:  
1st Time: \+1d6  
2nd Time: \+2d6  
3rd Time: \+2d6

## Completing Challenges

After all Blacksmiths have used all their Action Points, run out of valid actions, or Passed, Blacksmiths begin the Challenges phase. Each Blacksmith calculates their dice pool by adding all the dice up from Ingredients and any sharpening actions taken. Each Blacksmith faces the Strength Challenge and then the Sharpness Challenge. For each Challenge, each Blacksmith rolls their whole dice pool, applies any effects and modifiers, then takes any reactions they have. Penalties accrued from a Strength Challenge are applied to the Sharpness Challenge in that Quest but do not carry over to the next Quest. 

At the end of each Sharpness Challenge, each Blacksmith records their Successes and reserves one of their current Ingredients for all future rounds. After each Quest, each hero may reserve an Ingredient for reuse in subsequent Quests. All other Ingredients and the Challenges are discarded for the whole game. Selecting reserved Ingredients has the same action cost and may not be used by other Blacksmiths. All Action Points, dice pools, effects, and modifiers reset after each Quest. 

## Dice Rolling Details

For each Challenge, each Blacksmith rolls their whole dice pool, applies any effects and modifiers, then takes any reactions they have. Penalties accrued from a Strength Challenge are applied to the subsequent Sharpness Challenge. 

Die results are the individual value of each die. It is calculated by first applying Effects then Modifiers. After those calculations, Blacksmiths may take Reactions. Results may go below 1 or above 6\.

Final results are used to determine penalties in the Strength Challenge and Successes in the Sharpness Challenge.

Example: Player One has Hardened Steel, Gold, and Bronze against the Kraken for the Strength Challenge and then the Vampire Bat as the Sharpness Challenge. They roll 4d6(3 from Hardened Steel and 1 from Bronze): 2,5,5,6 for a total of 18\. Because they’re below 20 and don’t want a penalty from the Kraken, they choose to reroll the 2 and get a 3 for a total of 19\. Because the total is not greater than 20, they lose 1d6 for the Sharpness Challenge. They roll 3d6 now and get 2,2,6 for 1 Success.

## Determining the Victor

At the end of the third Quest, each Blacksmith adds up all their Successes from each Quest. The winner of the game is the Blacksmith with the most Successes.
